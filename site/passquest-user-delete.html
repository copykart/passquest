Delete your PassQuest account

Last updated: 9 September 2026

You can permanently delete your PassQuest account and every piece of data tied to it — directly from inside the app, in four steps. It takes about a minute.

Account deletion is permanent. Your profile, exam history, progress, saved work and subscription records are erased and cannot be recovered. If you only want a copy of your data, use Settings → Export Data first.

  1. Sign in to the PassQuest app

    Open PassQuest on your phone and sign in with the email and password for the account you want to delete.

  2. Open your account profile

    On the Home screen, tap your profile avatar in the top-right corner to open your account.

  3. Open Settings and tap Delete Account

    In Settings, scroll down to the Account Security section and tap Delete Account.

  4. Confirm account deletion

    Read the warning, then tap Delete Account in the confirmation dialog. This cannot be reversed.

What happens after you delete

  • Your profile and progress are removed from the app straight away.
  • All personal data is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days.
  • Anonymised, aggregate usage statistics that can no longer identify you may be retained.
  • An active subscription is not cancelled automatically — cancel it separately in Google Play or the App Store to avoid future charges.

Can’t sign in?

If you can no longer access the app, email support@passquest.app from the email address on the account and ask us to delete it. We confirm ownership, then complete deletion within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your data.
