If you are preparing for an English or admissions test, most of the advice online is either years out of date or written to sell you a course. This blog is our attempt at something more useful: clear, current guides from the team that builds [PassQuest](https://passquest.app), an AI exam-prep app with a predictive live score that tells you when you are actually ready.

## What you'll find here

We write in five buckets:

- **Exam guides** — how each test is scored, what each section asks of you, and what a good study plan looks like.
- **Comparisons** — honest, side-by-side breakdowns when you are choosing between two tests or two study methods.
- **Scholarship updates** — funding rounds, eligibility rules and deadlines, refreshed as they are announced.
- **Japa / relocation** — visa routes, permanent-residency pathways and the real cost of moving abroad.
- **Exam & travel news** — date changes, fee changes and format changes, published the week they happen.

## How we keep posts trustworthy

Every article carries a publish date, and a visible "updated" date whenever we revise it. When a claim depends on an official source — a test board, an embassy, a scholarship body — we link straight to it. If we get something wrong, we fix the post and note the change.

> Our goal is simple: you should be able to read one PassQuest guide and know your next step, without opening ten more tabs.

## Start here

If you are still deciding which test to take, the comparison guides are the fastest way in. If you already know your exam, head to its category page from the menu above — each one collects every guide, news post and deadline for that test in one place.
